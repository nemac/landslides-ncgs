About this map

Flagged areas show potential channelized debris flow pathways where the National Weather Service forecasts at least 5 inches of rain within a 24-hour period.

For more information, visit the Landslides in Western North Carolina home page. To see where landslides have been documented across the state, explore the Landslide Inventory of North Carolina.
